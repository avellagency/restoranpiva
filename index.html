Česta pitanjaFrequently asked questions

Koje je radno vrijeme?What are your opening hours?

Otvoreni smo svaki dan od 07:00 do 23:00. Služimo doručak, ručak i večeru.We are open every day from 07:00 to 23:00, serving breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Mogu li platiti karticom?Can I pay by card?

Da. Primamo gotovinu i kartice. Obrok je okvirno 10–15 € po osobi.Yes. We accept cash and cards. A meal is roughly €10–15 per person.

Primate li grupe i autobuse?Do you take groups and coaches?

Da. U formi za rezervaciju označite da dolazite kao grupa, ili nas pozovite na +382 67 306 740.Yes. Tick the group option in the booking form, or call us on +382 67 306 740.

Može li hrana za poneti?Can I take food with me?

Da. Ostatak hrane vam spakujemo za put.Yes. We pack whatever you don't finish for the road.

Ima li parking?Is there parking?

Za informacije o parkingu pozovite +382 67 306 740.Call +382 67 306 740 for parking information.

Ima li pristup za invalidska kolica?Is it wheelchair accessible?

Da, prostor ima pristup za invalidska kolica.Yes, the restaurant is wheelchair accessible.
